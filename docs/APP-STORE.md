# App Store notes

**Name:** Bud Relay
**Subtitle:** Plant now. Bloom later.
**Category:** Games › Puzzle (secondary: Casual)
**Age rating:** 4+

## Description

Plant a bud today and watch the whole bed open tomorrow.

Bud Relay is a relay puzzle about restoring a neighbourhood's gardens. Every bud shows how many care cycles it needs. When a flower blooms it hands care to its neighbours, and a bed you planned well opens in one long wave. Do you take a small relay now, or spend one more turn setting up a big one?

- Sixty levels across six places: a community garden, a rooftop, a schoolyard, a greenhouse market, a lakeside park, and an old botanical garden.
- Six flowers that behave differently. Daisies trigger, tulips relay hard, lavender brings bees, marigolds hold on through the heat.
- Weather that matters. Water on hot days, plant freely in the rain.
- Tools that work like tools: compost, mulch, fertilizer, shears.
- A garden that is yours. Place benches, paths, a birdbath, a bee hotel, and the flowers you grew. Neighbours turn up.
- Bouquet orders at the market, a greenhouse collection, and a Daily Bloom shared by everyone.
- No lives, no waiting, no ads, no account. Progress stays on your device.
