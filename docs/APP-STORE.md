# App Store notes

**Name:** Bud Relay
**Subtitle:** Plant now. Bloom later.
**Category:** Games › Puzzle (secondary: Casual)
**Age rating:** 4+

## Description

Plant a bud today and watch the whole bed open tomorrow.

Bud Relay is a relay puzzle about restoring a neighbourhood's gardens. Every bud shows how many care cycles it needs. When a flower blooms it hands care to its neighbours, and a bed you planned well opens in one long wave. Do you take a small relay now, or spend one more turn setting up a big one?

- Sixty levels across six places: a community garden, a rooftop, a schoolyard, a greenhouse market, a lakeside park, and an old botanical garden.
- Twelve flowers that behave differently. Daisies trigger, tulips relay hard, hydrangeas reach diagonally, lavender brings bees, and marigolds hold on through the heat.
- Drag-to-preview shows the coming relay before you plant; a long chain creates a Bloom Echo that restores your permanent garden.
- Weather that matters. Water on hot days, plant freely in the rain.
- Tools that work like tools: compost, mulch, fertilizer, shears.
- A five-area garden that grows with you. Place benches, paths, water features, trees, and the flowers you grew. Collect small rewards from the objects and activities you restore.
- Bouquet orders at the market, a flower encyclopedia and tree arboretum in the greenhouse, and a date-seeded Daily Bloom.
- No lives or energy gates on puzzle levels. No ads or account. Progress stays on your device.
