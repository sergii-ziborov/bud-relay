# Bud Relay — Player Guide

Bud Relay is a puzzle about setting up a chain of blooms and using its harvest to restore a shared neighbourhood garden. The puzzle bed and the permanent garden are different places: every level is played on a 5×5 bed; the estate grows between levels.

## Your first relay

1. Choose one of the three bud cards at the bottom of the screen. The small number is the care cycles it needs to bloom.
2. Drag it over an empty plot. While you hover, white rings and dotted links preview the blooms and relays that would happen on this turn. Release to plant. You can also tap a card and then tap a plot.
3. Planting spends one turn. Each watered bud counts down once. A flower that reaches zero blooms and gives care to nearby buds; any bud pushed to zero joins the same cascade.
4. Watch the goals above the bed. Complete them before the turn counter reaches zero. There are no lives to buy or energy gates on puzzle levels.

Usually care passes to the four orthogonal neighbours. Tulips and irises give **two** care points; hydrangeas reach all eight surrounding plots. A long relay can be built by placing slow buds near quick triggers and waiting until their timers line up.

You can spend a turn with **Tend** without planting. This can be the right move when several buds are one cycle from blooming. A bloomed flower is harvested after its stay on the bed; marigolds and asters stay two turns, while most kinds stay one.

## Bloom Echo

A connected relay of four or more flowers makes the bed burst into a wave of petals. It also earns **Bloom Echo**: a ×4 or ×5 chain gives one garden-care point, ×6 or ×7 gives two, and longer chains give more. The result screen shows the total. On a completed level, those points restore the least-recovered area you have opened in the permanent garden. Longer relays therefore change the place you are building, not just your score.

Relays of ×3, ×5, and ×8 also earn one, two, and three harvested flowers respectively for your basket. Lavender blooming with a pollinator-friendly partner can attract bees and earn seeds.

## Weather and tools

In rain, plots become wet. On hot days unprotected soil dries; a bud on dry soil pauses until watered. Marigolds protect nearby soil while blooming. Tools do not spend a turn:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Water | Wets a plot and its neighbours. |
| Compost | Enriches an empty plot; its next bud starts closer to blooming. |
| Fertilizer | Adds one stem to a bud's eventual harvest. |
| Mulch | Protects a plot from drying for the rest of the level. |
| Shears | Removes a plant when you need the plot back. |

Seed packets swap one card in your hand for a chosen flower. A hint points to a move, but you decide whether the immediate bloom or a later, longer chain helps your goals.

## Restore the estate

Open **Garden** from the home screen. The Community Courtyard is available first; Meadow, Orchard, Pond Garden, and Greenhouse Yard open at player levels 4, 8, 15, and 24. Each area starts with a few build sites and gains more as you level up. The clouded areas are future expansions, not empty slots to place into.

- Pinch or use the +/− controls to zoom, and drag the landscape to pan. The land, paths, plants, fog, and items stay together under one camera. The scope button returns to the starting view.
- Select a flower or piece from the inventory; compatible ground lights up. Flowers belong in soil beds, fruit trees in the orchard, a willow by water, and path stones on paths. Tap a highlighted spot to place it.
- Tap an existing item to inspect it, collect a ready reward, or pick it up. Picking up decor returns it to inventory; uprooting a flower returns it to your basket.
- Tap an area's badge to see its restoration, then use **Restore** when available. Small activities marked on the map also give care and sometimes coins, seeds, flowers, or tools. Some actions recover over time; the puzzle itself never needs you to wait.

Placed items improve their area over time. Blooms, trees, benches, water, and a bee hotel make the estate feel more lived in and can complete Garden Goals for coin rewards.

## Other places

**Market:** fill neighbours' bouquet orders for coins and sometimes decor; buy seeds, supplies, and garden pieces.

**Greenhouse:** browse twelve flower entries, six trees, their care and field notes, cosmetic variants, and the Green Neighbors journal. Discoveries accumulate as you play.

**Daily Bloom:** a date-seeded bed with its own streak and best relay. It is a local offline challenge, not an online leaderboard.

The game saves on the device. No account, ads, or network connection are required.
